# Privacy Policy — US Tax with Pavan

_Last updated: October 2026_

## Overview

US Tax with Pavan ("the app") is an educational app that helps users learn US taxation concepts and prepare for tax-related job interviews.

## Data collection

**The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

- No account or sign-up is required.
- No personal information (name, email, phone number, location) is requested or stored.
- No analytics or tracking software is included.
- No data is shared with third parties.

## Internet access

The app uses internet access only to open external links you choose to tap — specifically, links to the developer's YouTube channel, Telegram channels, and WhatsApp community pages. These are standard links that open in your browser or the respective app; US Tax with Pavan does not read or store anything from those platforms.

## Third-party services

Tapping a community link (YouTube, Telegram, WhatsApp) takes you to that service's own app or website, which has its own separate privacy policy. This policy covers only US Tax with Pavan itself.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, since it collects no data at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same location with a new "last updated" date.
